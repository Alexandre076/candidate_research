# Automated analysis conclusion

**Approximately one in ten analyzed candidates is explicitly associated with at
least one criminal record in the submitted documents. Criminal actions and
investigations predominate; among identifiable subjects, the most prominent are
crimes against honor, domestic violence, crimes of official responsibility,
threats, bodily injury, and offenses involving public administration.**

This conclusion is based on 588 candidates with records among 5,757 analyzed
candidates, approximately 10.2%. The pipeline identified 1,494 relationships
between candidates and case records.

“Criminal record” includes investigations, inquiries, and cases without a
conviction. A candidate's presence in these documents does not establish that
the person was convicted or is guilty. The results were produced by automated
extraction without individual human review of the cases.
